# 3D model credits

`fenrir-paladin.glb` is based on "Fallen Paladin in Corrupted Black Plate Armor"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fallen-paladin-in-corrupted-black-plate-armor-df3056caff7e4f079bd4eec0fa9bcd54)
by Pigcraft (https://sketchfab.com/s8819296), licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Changes (ours): metal parts recolored to gold and the greatsword's blade to steel with a gold central strip
(tools/model/sword_mask.py + tools/model/gold_recolor.py, see tools/model/README.md), mesh simplified and textures
compressed (gltf-transform). The winged-helm wings are procedural geometry added by the app (src/ui/viewer/scene.ts),
not part of the original model.
