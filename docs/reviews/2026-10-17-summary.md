# Reviews of 2026-10-17: what was fixed, what is left

Four rules audits (`/um_actually`), run in parallel:
- [arcane casters](um_actually-2026-10-17-arcane.md);
- [metamagic, Spell Mastery, Improved Familiar, mithral](um_actually-2026-10-17-metamagic.md);
- [combat and character core](um_actually-2026-10-17-combat.md);
- [other classes and character building](um_actually-2026-10-17-classes.md).

Plus an [architecture review](architecture-2026-10-17.md).

## Fixed (tests in `src/engine/__tests__/audit-2026-10-17.test.ts`, `generic.test.ts`, `hitPoints.test.ts`)

### Classes
- **Regression: the cleric's 1st-level choices (two domains, energy, deity weapon) and the swordsage's discipline focus were no longer offered.** They were lost in commit ddc6fd2 and are now restored, with a regression test.
- A druid in metal armor, or with a non-wooden shield, is flagged.
- The phylactery of undead turning gives +4 turning level. The cleric example now has turning level 20.
- Wild shape no longer leaks into the build checks.
- Prestige class requirements now count feats granted by class features (the monk's Improved Unarmed Strike, the War domain's Weapon Focus).
- A domain's +1 caster level applies to every spell the cleric casts, whatever the class (3.5 FAQ).
- Three examples knew one language too many; corrected.

### Combat
- Toughness taken twice gives +6 hp.
- Energy drain takes a number of negative levels (input on the effect).
- An overloaded character loses its Dex bonus to AC.
- The SRD reduced-speed table is used for other base speeds (50 → 35, 60 → 40…).
- The flat-footed toggle also means no attacks of opportunity.
- The ki damage line shows the same Power Attack factor as the engine.

### Hit points (new shared block, General and Combat panes)
- Damage takes temporary hp first, first-in first-out between sources (FAQ).
- Healing also removes as much nonlethal damage.
- Temporary hp count for the states.
- At 0 hp, nonlethal damage means unconscious.
- A stable character no longer shows as losing 1 hp per round.
- Steely resolve's delayed damage goes through temporary hp too.

### Arcane
- Spell Focus and Greater Spell Focus raise the save DC of their school.
- A specialist wizard's prohibited schools are off her list for scrolls, wands and staffs.
- Combat Casting gives +4 on Concentration (situational).
- Spells learned and Spell Mastery use permanent ability scores, so a fox's cunning or a headband no longer changes past picks.
- The bard example now carries a light load.
- The countersong text is corrected.

### Metamagic, Improved Familiar, data
- Widen Spell recognizes clouds and fogs (effect and target lines too).
- A domain spell in the cleric's domain slot is checked at its domain level.
- Improved Familiar requires being able to have a familiar.
- A familiar's spell resistance is the higher of its own and the master's level + 5.
- The mithral note now says proficiency is included.
- French feat names in the metamagic messages are corrected.
- The French imp stat block no longer lists fire resistance 20.

### House rules #38–#40 confirmed by the DM
- **#38:** the type/subtype familiars are offered.
- **#39:** the familiar's Int follows the table.
- **#40:** no metamagic spell in a slot the key ability is too low for.

## Left for later (minor; listed in the reports)
- **Ranger combat style:** a medium or heavy load should switch it off. This needs a reordering inside `compute()` (combat m2).
- **Familiar's master bonus:** no "within 1 mile" toggle; ruling C30 says there is one (arcane m2).
- **Cleric's spontaneous cure/inflict:** no metamagic option (metamagic m7).
- **Exotic Weapon Proficiency:** the Str 13 for the bastard sword and dwarven waraxe is not checked (classes m2).
- **Throwable melee weapons:** no thrown attack line (classes m5).
- **Monk's belt:** not computed (classes m6).
- The nitpicks in each report.

## Questions for the DM (pending house rules #41, #42)
- **#41:** a sorcerer or bard whose Charisma rises later. Does it learn the spells of a level it couldn't cast before?
- **#42:** for Improved Familiar's "arcane spellcaster level", which count applies: the highest caster level, or sorcerer and wizard levels together as for the familiar?
