# House rules & non-SRD register

Everything the playboard uses that is **not** plain 3.5 SRD. Only `confirmed` entries may be implemented.
`pending` entries need the user's (and DM's) decision first.

| # | Topic | Decision | Source | Status | Confirmed on |
|---|-------|----------|--------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Weapon Master / Maître d'armes prestige class | The only 3.0 content allowed. Data in `docs/rules/weapon-master.md` | *Sword and Fist* p. 38 / *De chair et d'acier* | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |
| 2 | All other 3.0 content (skills such as Scrutation, Sens de la nature, Langage secret…; feats such as Ambidextrie; the Excel sheet's formulas) | Dropped. 3.5 skills, feats and mechanics only | [3.5 skills](https://www.d20srd.org/indexes/skills.htm), [3.5 feats](https://www.d20srd.org/indexes/feats.htm) | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |
| 3 | Bonus feat granted by the character's deity | +1 feat on top of the SRD count: Weapon Focus (greatsword), granted by Scorpio, homebrew god of war | DM ruling | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |
| 4 | "Connaissances des monstres" | Homebrew Knowledge skill (DM). Key ability Int (the user's total +5 = 4 ranks + Int +1 matches). Class skill for Fighter, Weapon Master and Warblade (1 point per rank, max ranks = level + 3; warblade added 2026-10-15). Trained only (cannot be used without ranks), like the SRD Knowledge skills | DM ruling | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |
| 5 | Weight units | Store both lb and kg for every weight and load threshold, with **kg = lb ÷ 2 everywhere** (the French books' convention; their few printed values that differ, e.g. flask 2,25 kg or Str 10 light load 17,5 kg, are treated as typos — decided 2026-10-12). Load bands are computed in lb. Show lb in English and kg in French | [Carrying capacity](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/carryingCapacity.htm) | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |
| 6 | Weapon Master version | Original *Sword and Fist* version: class skills Intimidate, Knowledge (weaponry), Listen, Sense Motive, Spot; requires a masterwork weapon of the chosen type (book p. 39; corrected 2026-10-12 after reading the book). Knowledge (weaponry) is ignored (see #7) | *Sword and Fist* p. 38 | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |
| 7 | Knowledge (weaponry) | Listed as a Weapon Master class skill but not a 3.5 skill: ignored (not offered in the app) | *Sword and Fist* p. 38 | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |

| 9 | Hit dice | Never rolled: every level gives the maximum of the class hit die (d10 → 10 for Fighter and Weapon Master), + Con modifier | DM house rule; SRD default: [Hit points](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/combat/combatStatistics.htm#hitPoints) | confirmed | 2026-10-12 |
