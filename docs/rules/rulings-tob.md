# Rulings: *Tome of Battle* maneuvers (warblade, house rule #15)

Questions found while encoding the maneuvers. Researched 2026-10-15. Summaries in our own words; page numbers are the printed pages of *Tome of Battle*.

## Sources and their weight

| Source | Weight | Where |
|---|---|---|
| *Tome of Battle* text, incl. its general rules (Ch. 3, pp. 37–46) | RAW | book (`books/`, never committed) |
| Official ToB errata | none usable: the Wizards PDF actually holds *Complete Mage* errata | — |
| Official 3.5 FAQ (6/30/2008), ToB section pp. 74–76 | official, but silent on every question below | https://www.d20pfsrd.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/12/2017/01/Main35FAQv06302008.pdf |

| Forums (EN World, Giant in the Playground, Min-Max), dndtools | community | links per question |

**Result:** no official ruling exists for any of the nine questions. Six are settled by the book's own text; the three open ones were decided by the user as house rules #17–19 (2026-10-15).

## T1. Bounding Assault (Diamond Mind 4, p. 62): charge +2 / −2 AC? — **decided: house rule #17**
- Text: double move, then one melee attack at +2; it "is considered a charge" only to decide whether feats and abilities apply.
- When the book means a real charge, it says "you must make a charge attack", adds "in addition to the charge bonus" when bonuses stack (pp. 60, 61, 94), and spells out the −2 AC when it applies (p. 56). Bounding Assault says none of this.
- Recommended (RAW reading): **+2 on the attack, no second charge +2, no −2 AC**; counts as a charge for charge feats. Confidence medium-high on the +2, medium on the AC. Community discussion is thin (https://www.enworld.org/forum/showthread.php?171812).
- App: +2 on the strike's attack; −2 AC not applied.

## T2. Leaping Dragon Stance (Tiger Claw 3, p. 88): +10 on the check or +10 ft? — **decided: house rule #18 (+10 on the check)**
- Full entry: "+10-foot enhancement bonus on Jump checks"; the book's own summary list (p. 49): "+10 on Jump checks". Every jump counts as a running jump.
- Long jump: same result either way (check result = distance in feet). High jump: +10 on the check ≈ +2,5 ft (75 cm) of height; +10 ft of distance would be worth +40 on the check.
- Community split (https://www.enworld.org/threads/swooping-dragon-strike.192670/, https://www.dragonslair.it/forums/topic/57551-leaping-dragon-stance/).
- Recommended: **+10 enhancement bonus on Jump checks** (skill-bonus wording, matches the summary list, conservative). Confidence medium.
- App: +10 enhancement bonus on Jump while in the stance.

## T3. Wolf Fang Strike (Tiger Claw 1, p. 90): normal two-weapon penalties too? — **resolved**
- Text: one attack with each weapon against the same foe, −2 on each. The Bloodclaw Master entry (p. 97) calls this "the −2 penalty … for fighting with two weapons".
- Consensus (https://www.enworld.org/threads/wolf-fang-strike-maneuver-what-are-the-penalties.205530/): the −2 **replaces** the normal two-weapon penalties; no feat or light off-hand weapon needed; Improved/Greater Two-Weapon Fighting add nothing. Off-hand damage still uses ½ Str. Confidence high.

## T4. Missing prerequisite lines (Insightful Strike p. 63–64, Crushing Vise p. 82, Irresistible Mountain Strike p. 83, Mountain Tombstone Strike p. 84) — **resolved: none**
- The book and the official maneuver cards both print no prerequisite for these four. Customer service (unofficial) said Mountain Tombstone Strike is "intended as written".
- There is no formula in the book linking level to prerequisite (p. 44 only says some powerful maneuvers require others); about twenty other maneuvers also have none. Each maneuver's printed line is the rule.
- tob-tools.net shows "1 Stone Dragon maneuver" for Irresistible Mountain Strike: that comes from the fan-made *Unofficial Errata Project*, not Wizards. Ignored.

## T5. Lightning Throw (Iron Heart 8, p. 68): only 2 maneuvers? — **resolved: 2, as printed**
- Book, official card and dndtools all say two Iron Heart maneuvers. No errata or forum calls it a misprint; other 8th-level maneuvers also need two (Earthstrike Quake, Wolf Pack Tactics…).

## T6. Iron Heart Endurance (Iron Heart 6, p. 68): "your level" — **decided: house rule #19 (warblade level)**
- Only bare "your level" in the book; everywhere else ToB names the level it means (initiator, warblade, character level). A maneuver is not a class feature (anyone can learn it with Martial Study), so the FAQ's "class level for class features" rule doesn't fit.
- Recommended reading was character level (medium); the user ruled **warblade level** (house rule #19).
- Not relevant yet: a 6th-level maneuver needs initiator level 11.

## T7. Blood in the Water (Tiger Claw 1 stance, p. 86) — **resolved**
- +1 untyped on attack and damage per critical hit scored in the stance; it stacks with itself, no cap.
- Lost after 1 minute (10 rounds) without a critical hit; also lost when you change or end the stance, or become helpless (general stance rules, pp. 43, 45).
- App: a critical-hit counter in the Combat block (+N), reset when the stance changes.

## T8. Moment of Alacrity (Diamond Mind 6 boost, p. 64) — **resolved**
- Your **initiative count** (place in the order) improves by 20 from the next round for the rest of the encounter (pp. 45, 64); your initiative modifier doesn't change. Repeated use stacking: no ruling (low).

## T9. Steely Strike (Iron Heart 1, p. 69) — **resolved**
- +4 on that one melee attack; for 1 round (until just before your next action, p. 45) every opponent **except the one you struck** gets +4 on attack rolls against you. It is not an AC change: the app shows it as a situational −4 AC because, against those opponents, the result is the same.

## T10. Extra damage dice from a stance and a strike — **decided: house rule #20 (they add up)**
- ToB p. 40 says maneuver and stance *bonuses* on attack, damage, saves… don't stack. Strikes and stances that add dice say "extra … damage".
- Wizards customer service (unofficial, compiled at https://drammelsnotes.wikidot.com/the-unofficial-official-errata): Punishing Stance + Mountain Hammer dice stack, "additional damage die aren't considered bonuses". The official FAQ (p. 75) also calls Punishing Stance's +1d6 "extra damage". Community consensus agrees.
- App: extra dice from the stance and the strike are both listed; flat maneuver bonuses share one stacking source.

## T11. Maneuvers learned at the same level as prerequisites — **decided: house rule #21 (yes)**
- The book is silent. Customer service (unofficial, same compilation) answered "yes" to a warblade 1 picking, in one go, a maneuver with no prerequisite, one needing 1, one needing 2 and a stance needing 3. Same as the official FAQ ruling for feats gained at one level. A maneuver never counts toward itself.
- App: validated in pick order within the level.

## T12. Bolstering Voice / Leading the Attack and the initiator — **decided: house rule #22 (allies only)**
- Book: both name "your allies"; Lion's Roar says "you and allies" when it means both. FAQ p. 75 describes Leading the Attack as "your allies still gain the +4 morale bonus", and says White Raven Tactics doesn't work on the initiator.
- A customer-service answer said Bolstering Voice includes the user (reasoning from the bard, whose text explicitly says "including himself"). 3.5 has no general rule that you are your own ally.


# Crusader, swordsage and ToB prestige classes (researched 2026-10-16)

Open questions from `docs/rules/tob-classes.md` ("Doubts") and `docs/rules/tob-prestige.md` ("Open questions"). Same sources and weights as above, plus:
- **Official 3.5 FAQ (6/30/2008)**: *Crusader* (pp. 12–13), *Swordsage* (p. 26), *Warblade* (p. 26) and *Maneuvers and Stances* (pp. 74–76) sections: https://www.d20pfsrd.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/12/2017/01/Main35FAQv06302008.pdf
- **Wizards customer-service (CS) answers** (unofficial, compiled): https://drammelsnotes.wikidot.com/the-unofficial-official-errata

GiantITP threads could not be read (the site blocks automated access). EN World and other boards had little on these points.

Status words: **RAW** = the book's own text settles it; **FAQ** = official FAQ; **CS** = Wizards customer service (unofficial); **community** = forum consensus; **no answer** = nothing authoritative found.

## T13. Crusader steely resolve: does non-attack damage go into the pool? — **no answer (RAW leans "attacks only")**
- Text (p. 10): it triggers "when an opponent strikes you" / "when you are attacked" and stores the damage "the blow" deals. Special effects of the attack (poison, stun, energy drain) still apply at once.
- FAQ p. 12 only says that damage reduction and other reductions apply *before* damage enters the pool (if DR reduces it to 0, nothing is stored). It says nothing about spells or areas.
- Options:
  1. **Attacks only** (attack rolls, including touch attacks and ray spells): closest to "strikes you" and "the blow". This excludes fireball, falling, ongoing damage and failed Reflex saves.
  2. **Any damage from an opponent's action** (spells, breath weapons, area effects too): 3.5 often calls such effects "attacks" (the swordsage's own evasion, p. 17, talks about a Reflex save "against an attack"), and the FAQ talks about "incoming damage".
  3. Anything at all, including falling and the environment: nothing in the text supports this.
- No official or CS answer was found, and there is no clear forum consensus. https://www.enworld.org/threads/crusaders-delayed-damage-pool.207236/ only confirms that the damage is still taken.
- **App:** the user types any damage into "Take (delayed)", so the table decides case by case. The app enforces neither reading. Recommendation: keep this, and add a `pending` house rule so the DM picks option 1 or 2 once.

## T14. Steely resolve timing; damage left when the encounter ends — **no answer (RAW: "end of your next turn")**
- Text (p. 10): the stored total is taken "at the end of your next turn", and then the pool resets to 0. The pool "begins at 0 with each encounter".
- Damage stored during the crusader's own turn (an attack of opportunity while moving, a counter): read literally, her "next turn" is the following one, so the damage waits one more round. The other reading, "the next end of your turn" (the current turn), is also possible. There is no official ruling.
- End of the encounter: there is no official ruling. The text never says stored damage disappears. Time keeps running in rounds after a fight, so the crusader's "next turn" still comes and the damage is taken. On this reading, "begins at 0" just describes the normal state at the start of a fight. If the reset erased stored damage, a crusader could escape damage simply by ending the fight. Nobody on the boards we read argues for that reading.
- **App:** takes the pool at the end of *each* of the crusader's turns, so damage stored during her own turn is taken at the end of that same turn. It also takes the pool when the encounter ends. The end-of-encounter part matches the safe reading. The own-turn part is slightly *harsher* than the literal text. Recommendation: either keep it and record it as a house rule, or (closer to RAW) take damage stored during her own turn at the end of the following turn. Ask the user.

## T15. Furious counterstrike with an empty pool — **RAW: no bonus (app correct)**
- Text (pp. 10–11): bonus = pool ÷ 5, rounded down, "minimum +1", maximum +6. The book's own table starts at "1–9: +1" and has no row for 0.
- The "minimum +1" is needed because at 1st–3rd level the pool holds at most 5 points: without it, a pool of 1–4 would give +0. An empty pool holds no damage to channel (the feature is about attacks that struck you), and the table gives no bonus for it. Some online copies read it as "+1 always", but no official source says so.
- **App:** `min(6, max(1, floor(pool/5)))`, only when the pool is ≥ 1. Keep.

## T16. Crusader recovery: size of the new granted set at 10th/20th level — **RAW-supported: same as at the start of an encounter (app correct)**
- Text (p. 10): on recovery, "a new pair" of readied maneuvers is granted. At 10th and 20th level, "you begin an encounter" with one more granted maneuver (3, then 4). FAQ p. 13 restates recovery as "two of those maneuvers are randomly granted", but it answers a different question (what happens to maneuvers not yet expended) and doesn't consider 10th level.
- *Extra Granted Maneuver* (p. 30) treats the start of an encounter and the draw after recovery as the same number: a crusader normally begins an encounter or finishes recovering with two maneuvers granted. "Pair" is simply the wording for levels 1–9. There is no official answer for 10th level and above.
- **App:** the new draw uses the level's granted number (3 at 10th, 4 at 20th, +1 with Extra Granted Maneuver). Keep. Confidence medium. If the DM prefers, the strict reading is "always 2".

## T17. Swordsage AC bonus with no armor; "unencumbered" — **CS: light or no armor; SRD: unencumbered = light load (app correct)**
- Text (p. 16): Wis modifier to AC while you wear light armor, are unencumbered and use no shield.
- CS answer (unofficial, compilation above): the intent is that it works "whether you are wearing light armor or are unarmored". The question was passed to the errata team, but no errata ever came. It is not in the FAQ. Note: the swordsage's own evasion (p. 17) says "light armor or no armor", which a strict reader could use to argue the opposite.
- "Unencumbered" is not defined in the book. The SRD monk uses the same pair, "unarmored and unencumbered", and loses its AC bonus with a medium or heavy load (https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/monk.htm). So unencumbered = light load. CS also says this bonus doesn't stack with the monk's AC bonus (same name and effect).
- **App:** light or no armor, light load, no shield. Keep.

## T18. Swordsage sense magic: which level for the "level check"? — **CS: swordsage level**
- Text (p. 16): a "level check" against DC 10 + the item's caster level. The text doesn't say which level.
- CS answer (unofficial): you add your **swordsage level**. FAQ p. 26: one try per item; at the DM's discretion, another try after you gain a level. This also matches the FAQ's general rule that class features use the class level.
- **App:** should use d20 + swordsage level and note "one try per item (FAQ p. 26)". Check that the app shows this.

## T19. Swordsage insightful strike: strikes only? — **RAW: strikes only (app correct)**
- Text (p. 16): Wis modifier on damage "whenever you execute a strike" from the chosen discipline. Boosts, counters and stances aren't strikes (p. 5 defines the maneuver types).
- FAQ p. 26: it applies to every maneuver of that discipline, however you learned it (other classes, Martial Study). The second pick at 12th level must be a different discipline.
- **App:** strikes only. Keep. Check that the 12th-level pick refuses the discipline already chosen, and that maneuvers learned from other sources count.

## T20. One maneuver for several prestige class requirement entries — **no answer; the book's layout points to "distinct maneuvers" in two cases**
- Book: a stance is "a special type of maneuver" (p. 5). Stances count as maneuvers "for the purpose of meeting a prerequisite to learn a new maneuver" (p. 44). The book does **not** extend this to prestige class requirements. `tob-prestige.md` cites p. 43 and adds "prestige class and feat requirements", which goes further than the text and should be corrected.
- Shadow Sun Ninja (p. 126): one 2nd-level Setting Sun or Shadow Hand maneuver, one Setting Sun maneuver of any level and one Shadow Hand maneuver of any level. Three listed items naturally read as three maneuvers, but nothing forbids one maneuver filling two items.
- Deepstone Sentinel (p. 105): "two Stone Dragon maneuvers and one Stone Dragon stance". Because the stance is named as a separate item, the natural reading is three distinct picks: two maneuvers that are not stances, plus one stance. Jade Phoenix Mage (p. 114) puts maneuvers and stances on **separate lines** ("Martial Maneuvers" and "Martial Stances"), so the same reading applies.
- No FAQ, CS answer or forum consensus was found. One old GiantITP answer reads Shadow Sun Ninja as three maneuvers: https://forums.giantitp.com/archive/index.php/t-189272.html (seen only as a search snippet).
- **App:** each entry is checked on its own, so one maneuver can satisfy several entries. This is the permissive reading. Recommendation: for Deepstone Sentinel and Jade Phoenix Mage, don't count the required stance toward the maneuver count (stricter, follows the layout). For Shadow Sun Ninja, keep the current check or require three distinct maneuvers. These stay warnings either way. Ask the user and record a house rule.

## T21. Jade Phoenix Mage class skills — **no answer (kept as printed)**
- p. 115 prints exactly the Eternal Blade list (p. 110: Climb, Concentration, Diplomacy, Handle Animal, Intimidate, Jump, Martial Lore, Ride, Sense Motive, Tumble). Yet the class needs Knowledge (arcana), (history) and (religion) to enter, and it casts arcane spells. This is very likely a copy error, but no errata, FAQ or CS answer fixes it, and online reprints copy the printed list.
- **App:** as printed. Keep. If the user wants, a `pending` house rule could add Knowledge (arcana) and Spellcraft (the DM decides).

## T22. Desert Wind saves with no printed DC (Hatchling's Flame, Inferno Blast) — **FAQ: 10 + maneuver level + Wis modifier**
- The book prints only "Reflex half" for Hatchling's Flame (2nd level, p. 54) and Inferno Blast (9th level, p. 55). The general rules (p. 46) say each maneuver's description gives its DC.
- FAQ p. 75: when a maneuver allows a save but gives no DC, the DC is 10 + maneuver level + the key ability of its discipline: **Desert Wind = Wisdom** (Devoted Spirit Cha, Diamond Mind Str, Iron Heart Str, Setting Sun Str, Shadow Hand Wis, Stone Dragon Str, Tiger Claw Str, White Raven Int). CS gave the same answer for Inferno Blast.
- So **Hatchling's Flame: DC 12 + Wis modifier; Inferno Blast: DC 19 + Wis modifier**. These are exactly the values our notes guessed.
- **App:** in `maneuvers-desert-wind.json`, replace "DC not printed … ask the DM" with these DCs and cite FAQ p. 75. The same FAQ rule covers every other maneuver that has a save but no DC.

## T23. Bloodstorm Blade weapon aptitude — **RAW (app correct)**
- Warblade (p. 22): effective fighter level = warblade level − 2, and it stacks with real fighter levels. Bloodstorm Blade (p. 100): fighter level = (warblade + bloodstorm blade levels) − 2. This is one combined total with a single −2, not two separate −2s. The Bloodstorm Blade text doesn't repeat "stacks with actual fighter levels", but it extends the same warblade feature, so the stacking carries over. Both features also let you retrain single-weapon feats each morning. FAQ p. 26 says this covers Weapon Supremacy and the Weapon Focus from the swordsage's discipline focus.
- **App:** fighter levels + max(0, warblade + bloodstorm blade − 2). Keep.

## T24. Are there official ToB errata? — **effectively none**
- Wizards posted a "ToB errata" download, but after the first paragraphs the file actually held *Complete Mage* errata (https://forum.rpg.net/index.php?threads/3-5-tome-of-battle-book-of-9-swords-errata-needs-serious-errata.525909/). Customer service (compiled above) said it "will never be fixed", because Wizards ended 3.5 errata support. One forum post says a later copy held real ToB errata, but we found no copy of it.
- The only usable official rulings are in the 3.5 FAQ (pp. 12–13, 26, 74–76). CS answers are unofficial but show the designers' intent. The fan-made "Unofficial Errata Project" does not come from Wizards.

### Side findings
- Swordsage skill points: the book prints "(6 + Int) ×6 at 1st level" (p. 16). CS confirmed this is a misprint for the normal ×4. The app already uses ×4.
- CS (unofficial): a prestige class adds its full levels to initiator level only if its text says so. Otherwise it adds half its levels.
