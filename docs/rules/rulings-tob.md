# Rulings: *Tome of Battle* maneuvers (warblade, house rule #15)

Questions found while encoding the maneuvers. Researched 2026-10-15. Summaries in our own words; page numbers are the printed pages of *Tome of Battle*.

## Sources and their weight

| Source | Weight | Where |
|---|---|---|
| *Tome of Battle* text, incl. its general rules (Ch. 3, pp. 37–46) | RAW | book (`books/`, never committed) |

**Result:** no official ruling exists for any of the nine questions. Six are settled by the book's own text; the three open ones were decided by the user as house rules #17–19 (2026-10-15).

## T1. Bounding Assault (Diamond Mind 4, p. 62): charge +2 / −2 AC? — **decided: house rule #17**
- Text: double move, then one melee attack at +2; it "is considered a charge" only to decide whether feats and abilities apply.
- When the book means a real charge, it says "you must make a charge attack", adds "in addition to the charge bonus" when bonuses stack (pp. 60, 61, 94), and spells out the −2 AC when it applies (p. 56). Bounding Assault says none of this.
- Recommended (RAW reading): **+2 on the attack, no second charge +2, no −2 AC**; counts as a charge for charge feats. Confidence medium-high on the +2, medium on the AC. Community discussion is thin (https://www.enworld.org/forum/showthread.php?171812).
- App: +2 on the strike's attack; −2 AC not applied.

## T2. Leaping Dragon Stance (Tiger Claw 3, p. 88): +10 on the check or +10 ft? — **decided: house rule #18 (+10 on the check)**
- Full entry: "+10-foot enhancement bonus on Jump checks"; the book's own summary list (p. 49): "+10 on Jump checks". Every jump counts as a running jump.
- Long jump: same result either way (check result = distance in feet). High jump: +10 on the check ≈ +2,5 ft (75 cm) of height; +10 ft of distance would be worth +40 on the check.
- Community split (https://www.enworld.org/threads/swooping-dragon-strike.192670/, https://www.dragonslair.it/forums/topic/57551-leaping-dragon-stance/).
- Recommended: **+10 enhancement bonus on Jump checks** (skill-bonus wording, matches the summary list, conservative). Confidence medium.
- App: +10 enhancement bonus on Jump while in the stance.

## T3. Wolf Fang Strike (Tiger Claw 1, p. 90): normal two-weapon penalties too? — **resolved**
- Text: one attack with each weapon against the same foe, −2 on each. The Bloodclaw Master entry (p. 97) calls this "the −2 penalty … for fighting with two weapons".
- Consensus (https://www.enworld.org/threads/wolf-fang-strike-maneuver-what-are-the-penalties.205530/): the −2 **replaces** the normal two-weapon penalties; no feat or light off-hand weapon needed; Improved/Greater Two-Weapon Fighting add nothing. Off-hand damage still uses ½ Str. Confidence high.

## T4. Missing prerequisite lines (Insightful Strike p. 63–64, Crushing Vise p. 82, Irresistible Mountain Strike p. 83, Mountain Tombstone Strike p. 84) — **resolved: none**
- The book and the official maneuver cards both print no prerequisite for these four. Customer service (unofficial) said Mountain Tombstone Strike is "intended as written".
- There is no formula in the book linking level to prerequisite (p. 44 only says some powerful maneuvers require others); about twenty other maneuvers also have none. Each maneuver's printed line is the rule.
- tob-tools.net shows "1 Stone Dragon maneuver" for Irresistible Mountain Strike: that comes from the fan-made *Unofficial Errata Project*, not Wizards. Ignored.

## T5. Lightning Throw (Iron Heart 8, p. 68): only 2 maneuvers? — **resolved: 2, as printed**
- Book, official card and dndtools all say two Iron Heart maneuvers. No errata or forum calls it a misprint; other 8th-level maneuvers also need two (Earthstrike Quake, Wolf Pack Tactics…).

## T6. Iron Heart Endurance (Iron Heart 6, p. 68): "your level" — **decided: house rule #19 (warblade level)**
- Only bare "your level" in the book; everywhere else ToB names the level it means (initiator, warblade, character level). A maneuver is not a class feature (anyone can learn it with Martial Study), so the FAQ's "class level for class features" rule doesn't fit.
- Recommended reading was character level (medium); the user ruled **warblade level** (house rule #19).
- Not relevant yet: a 6th-level maneuver needs initiator level 11.

## T7. Blood in the Water (Tiger Claw 1 stance, p. 86) — **resolved**
- +1 untyped on attack and damage per critical hit scored in the stance; it stacks with itself, no cap.
- Lost after 1 minute (10 rounds) without a critical hit; also lost when you change or end the stance, or become helpless (general stance rules, pp. 43, 45).
- App: a critical-hit counter in the Combat block (+N), reset when the stance changes.

## T8. Moment of Alacrity (Diamond Mind 6 boost, p. 64) — **resolved**
- Your **initiative count** (place in the order) improves by 20 from the next round for the rest of the encounter (pp. 45, 64); your initiative modifier doesn't change. Repeated use stacking: no ruling (low).

## T9. Steely Strike (Iron Heart 1, p. 69) — **resolved**
- +4 on that one melee attack; for 1 round (until just before your next action, p. 45) every opponent **except the one you struck** gets +4 on attack rolls against you. It is not an AC change: the app shows it as a situational −4 AC because, against those opponents, the result is the same.

## T10. Extra damage dice from a stance and a strike — **decided: house rule #20 (they add up)**
- ToB p. 40 says maneuver and stance *bonuses* on attack, damage, saves… don't stack. Strikes and stances that add dice say "extra … damage".
- Wizards customer service (unofficial, compiled at https://drammelsnotes.wikidot.com/the-unofficial-official-errata): Punishing Stance + Mountain Hammer dice stack, "additional damage die aren't considered bonuses". The official FAQ (p. 75) also calls Punishing Stance's +1d6 "extra damage". Community consensus agrees.
- App: extra dice from the stance and the strike are both listed; flat maneuver bonuses share one stacking source.

## T11. Maneuvers learned at the same level as prerequisites — **decided: house rule #21 (yes)**
- The book is silent. Customer service (unofficial, same compilation) answered "yes" to a warblade 1 picking, in one go, a maneuver with no prerequisite, one needing 1, one needing 2 and a stance needing 3. Same as the official FAQ ruling for feats gained at one level. A maneuver never counts toward itself.
- App: validated in pick order within the level.

## T12. Bolstering Voice / Leading the Attack and the initiator — **decided: house rule #22 (allies only)**
- Book: both name "your allies"; Lion's Roar says "you and allies" when it means both. FAQ p. 75 describes Leading the Attack as "your allies still gain the +4 morale bonus", and says White Raven Tactics doesn't work on the initiator.
- A customer-service answer said Bolstering Voice includes the user (reasoning from the bard, whose text explicitly says "including himself"). 3.5 has no general rule that you are your own ally.
