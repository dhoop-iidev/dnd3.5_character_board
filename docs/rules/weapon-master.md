Non-SRD content, allowed by house-rules #1. Store numbers and short summaries in our own words only: never copy the book text into the repo.

Sources:
- EN: *Sword and Fist* (3.0), pp. 38–39 — **verified against the user's copy (`books/Sword & Fist.pdf`, 2026-10-12)**. Summary: https://dndtools.org/classes/weapon-master/
- FR: *De chair et d'acier*, revised in *Guide de l'Orient* and *Recueil de visions épiques*. Summary: https://corpus-draconique.com/wiki/doku.php?id=classe:maitre_d_armes

Status: requirements, table and features below verified against the book (pp. 38–39). Rulings: rulings-character.md#q10.

## Entry requirements
- Base attack bonus +5
- Intimidate (Intimidation) 4 ranks
- Feats: Weapon Focus (chosen weapon), Combat Reflexes, Expertise (3.5: Combat Expertise; see rulings-character.md#q10-conversion), Dodge, Mobility (Souplesse du serpent), Whirlwind Attack (which itself needs Spring Attack)
- Dex 13 (listed with the feats, p. 39)
- Proficient with the chosen weapon
- **Masterwork weapon** of the chosen type (unless unarmed), p. 39. Any masterwork (or magic) weapon of that type works, not only the one owned when entering the class (p. 38). Fenrir's greatsword +2 qualifies (magic weapons are masterwork).

## Class data
- Hit die: d10
- Skill points: 2 + Int modifier per level (+1 for humans, SRD)
- Class skills (original *Sword and Fist* version, house-rules #6): Intimidate, Listen, Sense Motive, Spot (Knowledge (weaponry) ignored: house-rules #7)

## Feature summaries (details: rulings-character.md#q10)
- **Weapon of choice:** one melee weapon type (the one with Weapon Focus); the class features only work with a masterwork weapon of that type; can't be changed (p. 38). Resolved: see rulings-character.md#q10-weapon-of-choice
- **Ki damage:** after a hit, deal the weapon's maximum normal damage (weapon dice max + normal modifiers such as Str) instead of rolling; extra damage (e.g. Power Attack, sneak attack) is rolled/added normally, not maximized; can't be used on a critical hit (p. 39). Uses per day = class level. Resolved: see rulings-character.md#q10-ki-damage
- **Increased multiplier:** on a critical hit, the critical multiplier is +1 (declare before rolling damage). Uses per day as in the table. Resolved: see rulings-character.md#q10-increased-multiplier
- **Superior Weapon Focus:** +1 more on attack rolls with the weapon of choice (stacks with Weapon Focus and Greater Weapon Focus). Resolved: see rulings-character.md#q10-superior-weapon-focus
- **Superior Combat Reflexes:** total attacks of opportunity per round = Dex mod + Wis mod. Book wording confirmed (p. 39); interaction with Combat Reflexes: house-rules #12 (better of the two).
- **Ki critical:** gain Improved Critical with the weapon; if already owned, +2 threat range instead. The book says the +2 is applied **last, after** Improved Critical or *keen* (p. 39), so it does extend those (e.g. 17–20 → 15–20).
- **Ki Whirlwind Attack:** Whirlwind Attack as a standard action instead of a full-round action. Only one Whirlwind attack per round (p. 39). Combat Expertise with it: still open (DM), not before WM 9.
